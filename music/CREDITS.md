# Music

"Mist City" by Section7 at OpenGameArt
(https://opengameart.org/content/mist-city), licensed CC-BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

`mist_city.ogg` is the original as it was downloaded. Changes: the
pieces in `loops/` were cut out of it at its bar lines and joined end to
start so that they loop, some low-passed, high-passed and given an echo,
and two endings faded out (`tools/music_loops.py` makes them).
