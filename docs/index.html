PIXALORIEN

Three Pixaloriens land on a desert planet. Far below its surface, in a sealed vault, lies a treasure. Dig through a living world of falling sand, water, gas and lava — and bring it back to the ship. All three of them, ideally.

The game

Pixalorien is a single-player game of real time with pause — and with going back, when a plan goes wrong. The world is a falling-sand simulation 2048 by 1024 cells: dunes and mesas on the surface, chambers, lakes, sealed pockets of gas and lava below, and somewhere near the bottom, the vault.

Your three Pixaloriens walk, climb, swim, grapple and jetpack through what they can see; the rest of the world is dark until a helmet lamp or a flare lights it. Cut rock, flood a shaft, drop a bridge of sand — the sand keeps its own counsel, and cave-ins, gas and lava punish the hasty.

Order each Pixalorien yourself, or hand the digging to a strategy: cautious feels its way through known ground, brute force cuts straight for the treasure. Switch any time — then take back over when the strategy meets something it did not expect.

  • Three crew, one treasure
    Everyone who goes down should come back up.
  • A world that flows
    Sand, water, gas, smoke and lava simulated cell by cell.
  • Darkness is real
    You only know what your crew has seen lit.
  • Pause, speed up, rewind
    Space pauses; the timeline can be wound back.
  • Manual, cautious or brute
    Play it yourself or supervise a strategy (F5–F7).
  • A new world every seed
    Procedurally dug planets, no two alike.
